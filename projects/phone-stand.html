Phone Stand

SolidWorks CAD Ergonomics Product Design Completed

An adjustable desktop phone stand engineered for long-session ergonomic comfort. The design balances optimal viewing angle, thermal airflow, and cable management in a compact, printable form-factor.

  1. 01

    Ergonomic Research

    Reviewed ISO 9241 display guidelines and surveyed target users for posture pain-points during prolonged mobile use.

  2. 02

    Concept Sketching

    Generated 12+ initial concepts exploring folding mechanisms, material choices, and angle-locking strategies.

  3. 03

    CAD Modelling — SolidWorks

    Detailed parametric model with assembly constraints, interference checks, and print-tolerance allowances built in.

  4. 04

    Prototype & Iterate

    FDM-printed in PLA, tested across four phone form-factors, refined hinge geometry across three revision cycles.
